# Programmes, research, teaching, and access paths

These representative workflows were exercised in connected Chrome on 6 October 2026. Use their current visible controls and linked destinations; counts, schedules, and access requirements can change. This is not a complete content inventory.

## Event Database: year windows, map cities, and pagination

At [Event Database](https://aaa.org.hk/en/collections/event-database), the From and To dropdowns immediately apply year constraints. Check filter chips and the resulting rows: the dropdowns themselves reset to their placeholder captions. Searching China Avantgarde and selecting From 1993 returned its four-stop tour. Adding To 1993 produced no records; To 1994 restored it, including the Odense stop ending 6 February 1994. A narrow year window can therefore miss an event with a relevant stop inside that year. For overlapping-period research, widen the window and inspect venue/date pairs; this test does not establish the engine's full date-matching specification.

No visible city text input or city autocomplete was present. City selection worked through the map: expand clusters until individual markers appear, click the marker, then click its city-name popup. Merely opening the Berlin(1) popup did not apply a filter. Clicking that name produced a CITY:BERLIN, GERMANY chip and [this committed search](https://aaa.org.hk/en/collections/event-database/search/cities:berlin-germany--year-to:1994--year-from:1993--keywords:china-avantgarde). The result still included all four tour venues, so a city filter selects event entities rather than reducing each event to one venue. Scroll outside the map to move the page; wheel input over the map changes the map view.

Event (A–Z) sorting retained the city, year, and keyword chips and added `/sort/event:asc` to the observed URL. Removing the keyword and upper year through their chips produced a [Berlin/From-1993 query](https://aaa.org.hk/en/collections/event-database/search/cities:berlin-germany--year-from:1993/sort/event:asc) with ten initial event rows. One Load more action eventually appended ten more and changed the list URL to `/page/2`; the first immediate observation still showed ten. Count event entities rather than raw links: each row has repeated links for its venues/dates, and appended detail links can carry different search/page context. Follow actual links and loading controls instead of constructing endpoints. Only one additional batch was tested; this was not an inventory of all Berlin events.

## Programmes: filters have different behaviours

At [Programmes](https://aaa.org.hk/en/programmes), selecting a Year or Type in the visible custom dropdown immediately applies a search. Check the resulting filter chips, URL, and cards. The dropdown captions can return to “Year” and “Type” even while the active chips remain below them.

The video icon's checkbox has a tooltip, “hide results without video”. It combines with Year and Type. A successful representative search selected 2020, Talk, and video, producing [this result](https://aaa.org.hk/en/programmes/programmes/search/year:2020--has-video:on--type:talk). Nine cards initially appeared; Load more exposed all twelve and then hid itself, without changing the address. Follow the actual loading controls; do not derive pagination endpoints.

**Series behaves differently.** Selecting Teaching Labs from a year-filtered page opened the separate [Teaching Labs series page](https://aaa.org.hk/en/programmes/programmes/teaching-labs), with programmes from several years. The previous year constraint was no longer applied. Read the series description and manually inspect its programme dates when needed; do not report a combined series/year filter unless the live page proves it.

Programme listings separate current/upcoming and past groups. Dates may describe application deadlines, residencies spanning years, or individual sessions. Open the detail page before recommending attendance. The [Reflections on Modern Art Histories programme](https://aaa.org.hk/en/programmes/programmes/reflections-on-modern-art-histories-in-and-across-africa-south-and-southeast-asia/search/year:2020--has-video:on--type:talk) displayed a Play Video control, recording caption, date/time, language, moderator, and speakers. This verifies a video-bearing detail page; playback and transcript availability require their own checks.

## Shortlists: editorial source to library record

[Shortlists](https://aaa.org.hk/en/collections/shortlists) are dated thematic selections with contributor annotations. Open a shortlist and read its scope, author, and date before following its recommendations. Links can be embedded in location codes rather than book titles.

For example, [Hong Kong Artist-Run Independent Spaces](https://aaa.org.hk/en/collections/shortlists/hong-kong-artist-run-independent-spaces), by Sophia Lam, dated 5 June 2025, describes its survey as non-exhaustive. Its recommended-reading code REF.CHL10 led to [The First Artist Village in Hong Kong](https://aaa.org.hk/en/collections/search/library/the-first-artist-village-in-hong-kong-oil-street-and-cattle-depot). The target supplied author, 2022 publication date, language, identifiers, and “Onsite” access with a Hong Kong Room location. The online shortlist is readable; that does not make every recommended book readable online.

## Learning resources: read the resource and its attachment

[Resources](https://aaa.org.hk/en/learning-participation/resources) separates Artist Exercises, Lesson Plans, Learn with Archives, and Archiving Handbook. Choose the relevant type, then inspect the author/developer, publishing date, intended audience, objectives, and source links. A programme announcement may lead to reusable teaching material, but its registration status is separate.

The [Mapping Connections lesson resource](https://aaa.org.hk/en/learning-participation/resources/lesson-plans/mapping-connections-empowering-art-education-through-the-archive) credits Peggy Kwan and Carmen Kwok and gives a publishing date of 3 July 2026. Its [Graph Commons User Guide](https://cdn.aaa.org.hk/_source/lp_documents/graph-commons-user-guide.pdf) downloaded and yielded five readable pages. Page 1 explains the map's thematic organisation; page 5 explains finding AAA reference links and location codes through artist nodes. This supports a teaching discovery workflow, not an exhaustive AAA search or an independent assertion about each mapped artist.

The page and PDF linked different Graph Commons graph IDs. Do not silently treat them as identical. Follow and verify the particular external map needed for the task, retain its source link, and resolve differing links before claiming they expose the same dataset. External map interaction was not tested in this verification.

## Authored research: inspect the body, not just the card

[LIKE A FEVER essays](https://aaa.org.hk/en/like-a-fever/like-a-fever/type/essays) lead to full articles with an Imprint. [The Masterpiece is the Archive](https://aaa.org.hk/en/like-a-fever/like-a-fever/the-masterpiece-is-the-archive/type/essays) displayed author Sezin Romi, topic Essays, date 5 March 2025, notes, and a statement about earlier Turkish versions. Keep the current article's date and language/version history distinct. List-page blurbs are not substitutes for reading the argument.

[Papers & Presentations](https://aaa.org.hk/en/grants/the-robert-h-n-ho-family-foundation-greater-china-research-grant-papers) can offer full HTML research papers rather than PDF downloads. The card's View link opened [Canton Express: A Regional/Spatial Model](https://aaa.org.hk/en/grants/the-robert-h-n-ho-family-foundation-greater-china-research-grant-papers/canton-express-a-regionalspatial-model), with introduction, three sections, numbered notes, and DUAN Ziying as author. Cite the paper and relevant section/note; do not borrow the date of a related grantee announcement or talk as the paper's publication date when none is displayed.

## Collection scope and restricted access

Read the global [Collections Overview](https://aaa.org.hk/en/collections/overview) for collecting scope and general access context. It distinguishes Research and Library Collections and explains that AAA is the custodian rather than automatically the copyright owner. Read individual collection information as well.

On [Space II Archive](https://aaa.org.hk/en/collections/search/archive/space-ii-archive), the Information control exposed Historical Notes, Scope and Content, Dates (Inclusive), Languages, Collection Access, Collection Use, and Archival History. It explicitly calls the collection non-exhaustive. Its material dates, processing dates, digitisation date, and launch date describe different stages. Record collection-specific access instructions alongside item restrictions, without generalising them to all AAA collections.

The [Alain Sayag interview](https://aaa.org.hk/en/collections/search/archive/john-clark-archive/object/interview-with-alain-sayag-july-2003/sort/title-asc) was verified live as Restricted. It requires the interviewee's written permission and displays an application instruction at least five working days before a visit. Its Application for Access link opened the current Microsoft form for Access to/Reproduction of AAA Research Collections. “Start now” exposed a first step with Name and Email Address required; company/organisation, job title, and phone were additional fields. No values were entered and nothing was submitted. Later form requirements were not inspected because they lie beyond this personal-information step.

Obtain the current application link from the item or collection page. Reading public instructions is sufficient for research; do not fill an application, invent personal data, or contact staff merely to discover later form steps. Explain the remaining verification boundary when it affects visit planning.
