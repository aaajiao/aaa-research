# Vocabulary and name expansion

## Source and age

On 6 October 2026, AAA's live [keyword browse page](https://aaa.org.hk/en/collections/browse/keywords-120) linked this [official keyword PDF](https://cdn.aaa.org.hk/_source/Collection_documents/keywords-forpdf20190506.pdf), whose filename identifies a **6 May 2019** version. Its text was readable. Prefer the document linked by that live page on future runs.

An [older 3 October 2018 PDF](https://cdn.aaa.org.hk/_source/Collection_documents/keywords-forpdf20181003.pdf) also remains accessible; it is historical, not the latest link observed. Neither version proves the live engine's matching behaviour. The small selection below was checked against the 2019 text.

The document separates indexing labels, alternatives, translations, related concepts, and cross-references. Preserve those distinctions. Broader and associated concepts can expand a search; they are not necessarily synonyms.

## A small set of useful lookup labels

The labels below come from the official reference. The explanations are guidance for constructing queries, not claims that every spelling is automatically matched by the live search.

| User's topic | Candidate label | How to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 双年展、三年展 | perennial exhibition | Useful when a recurring exhibition is indexed under a shared category; also search the event's own title and edition. |
| 网络艺术 | internet art | The reference also supplies web art and net art as alternative wording. Search observed work and artist names alongside this concept. |
| 数码／数位／数字艺术 | digital art | Use the first two Chinese forms as source-supported alternatives; the third is a common user wording to retain. Related technical practices are leads, not equivalent media classifications. |
| 艺术家自主经营的空间 | artist run space | Expand a broad independent-space inquiry toward who operates the space, without assuming every independent venue is run by artists. |
| 口述历史 | oral history | Useful for locating interview-based historical material; check whether the actual accessible evidence is audio, video, or a transcript. |
| 录像艺术 | video art | Keep this practice distinct from video used to document a different artwork or event. |

For technology-related research, the source also links the digital category with generative art and new media art. Use these as separate exploratory searches and report when they widen the question. A related-term match alone should not classify an artist's entire practice.

## Names, scripts, and subject relationships

1. Begin with the user's spelling. Add traditional/simplified Chinese, romanisation, historical names, and aliases only as query candidates; confirm identity against records before merging results.
2. Prefer variants actually displayed in a record's names or alternative-title fields. Chinese transliterations, Westernised name order, hyphenation, and initials can vary. A plausible transliteration is a lead, not an established alias.
3. Keep artists, collectives, venues, publishers, and organisers distinguishable even when they share a name. The official reference explains that group names belong to the site's people records rather than solely to movement keywords.
4. For an organisation, inspect its role in the record as well as topic keywords. The vocabulary's organisational categories describe material about an institution or space; the institution may instead appear through publishing or organising relationships.
5. Preserve differences between the source's translations, a historical title, a cataloguer's translated title, and your own explanatory translation. Show both scripts when that helps identification.

For a comprehensive enquiry, language expansion also includes the interface: the same aaajiao query produced different English and Traditional Chinese result sets in verification. Search the relevant interfaces, reapply filters after switching, and verify counterparts and broken links as described in [search.md](search.md). Merely translating one keyword in the English interface is not proof of equivalent coverage.

## A bounded expansion sequence

Try the exact name or title, then one verified spelling/script variant, then a suitable indexing label, then one broader or related concept. Combine each with the necessary period, place, or material type using the controls actually available. If the interface cannot express a constraint, inspect results against it rather than implying that the query enforced it.

For an empty result, relax one condition at a time and keep the original scope visible. For excessive results, distinguish responsibility roles, record types, and dates before adding more conceptual synonyms. Do not claim exhaustive coverage from a single wording or from search-engine indexing alone.
